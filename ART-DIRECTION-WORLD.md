# SIDE A — A world of sound

The YouTube reference uses a layered landscape with typography and foreground elements moving at different depths. This version applies that idea to a surreal listening-bar world rather than copying its travel subject.

## Implementation

- Full-bleed generated landscape, pointer parallax and scroll-driven camera push.
- Three.js foreground records at different depths, continuous record-to-platter transition, assembled turntable, exploded speaker and paired sound system.
- Playfair Display editorial typography; peach, burgundy, terracotta and warm ivory palette.
- Expanding listening-room reveal, word-by-word manifesto, playable eight-note keyboard with synthesized harmonics and a responsive waveform.
- Record sleeve tilt, user-initiated audio, motion pause, reduced-motion handling, native navigation and session dialogs.

## Generated artwork

Tool: built-in image generation. Source: `dist/assets/sound-world.webp` (optimized from the generated PNG). No generated video is used; the motion is rendered in the browser.

Prompt: Wide cinematic full-bleed background artwork for an immersive high-end music website, 16:9 landscape. An extraordinary surreal analog sound world at sunset: a still pale-peach sea, sculpted coral-red and terracotta rock islands, on the far right an enormous elegant sculptural brass gramophone horn growing from a rust-red rock formation, several ivory acoustic arches on a distant island to the far left, tiny glossy black stepping stones across reflective water, very distant lavender mountains, enormous soft cream sun in hazy pink sky. A few abstract chrome spherical objects floating near distant horizon. Premium surreal architectural photography mixed with sophisticated 3D editorial art, tactile stone, polished brass, soft volumetric light, realistic materials, dreamlike gravity. Palette burnt red, dusty blush, pale peach, burgundy shadows and brass. Horizon at 57%, top half mostly clean peach sky with subtle clouds for large dark title overlay, important architectural objects at left and right edges, center open for live 3D object. Foreground reflective water and dark red low rock shelf entering bottom right. Atmospheric perspective. No letters, text, branding, UI, people or vinyl records in artwork.

SIDE A remains a fictional design concept, not a physical venue or bookable event.
